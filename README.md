## 📐 Product Range & Technical Specifications

### 1. PUF Panel (Polyurethane Foam)
- **Insulation Core**: Rigid Polyurethane Foam (PUF)
- **Width – Wall**: 1200 mm (Customized available)
- **Width – Roof**: 1000 mm Standard / Customized
- **Core Thicknesses**: 40, 50, 60, 80, 100 mm
- **Fascia Options**: PPGL / PPGI / SS / AL (Galvalume, Galvanized Iron, Stainless Steel 304, Aluminium)
- **Core Density**: 40 kg/m³ (±2)
- **Melting Point**: > 150°C
- **Fire Class**: Fire Retardant (Self-extinguishing)

### 2. Rockwool Panel (Basalt Mineral Wool)
- **Insulation Core**: High-Density Rockwool / Mineral Wool
- **Width – Wall**: 1200 mm
- **Width – Roof**: 1000 mm Standard
- **Core Thicknesses**: 50, 60, 80, 100, 120 mm
- **Fascia Options**: PPGL / PPGI / SS / AL
- **Core Density**: 90 kg/m³ standard / 100–120 kg/m³
- **Melting Point**: **> 1000°C** (Extreme Non-Combustible Fire Barrier)
- **Sound Reduction**: **28 – 30 dB** Acoustic Decibel Damping
- **Fire Class**: Fire Resistant (Non-combustible A1 classification)
