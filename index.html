PUF & Rockwool Technical FAQ

Common technical questions regarding thickness selection, core materials, and ordering from infrabuildcon2015.

Choose PUF Panels (40 kg/m³) when your primary requirement is maximum thermal insulation, energy reduction for cold rooms, warehouses, and cleanrooms. Choose Rockwool Panels (90–120 kg/m³) when non-combustible fire resistance (>1000°C melting point) or acoustic sound reduction (28–30 dB) is needed, such as in generator rooms, boiler zones, or public facilities.
  • PUF Panels: Wall width 1200 mm; Roof width 1000 mm. Thicknesses: 40, 50, 60, 80, 100 mm.
  • Rockwool Panels: Wall width 1200 mm; Roof width 1000 mm. Thicknesses: 50, 60, 80, 100, 120 mm.
We provide PPGL (Pre-painted Galvalume), PPGI (Pre-painted Galvanized Iron), SS (Stainless Steel 304 food-grade), and AL (Aluminium). Each can be supplied with protective peel-off guard film.
